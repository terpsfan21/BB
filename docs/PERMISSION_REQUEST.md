# Asking an odds screen for permission to poll it

4codds.com and betwatch.io both restrict automated collection (see `DATA_SOURCES.md`). Automating
either needs the owner's written permission. A message along these lines can be sent from your own
account (4codds: its Discord or X account; Betwatch: its support contact):

> I'm an individual bettor running a small personal research project on college basketball totals.
> I'd like to record your displayed totals and prices for NCAAB games about once every 15–30 minutes
> during the season, for my own analysis only — no redistribution, no resale, no public display.
> Is there a supported way to do that (an API, an export, or permission to read the same data your
> board loads)? I'm happy to follow any rate limit you set and to identify my requests however you
> prefer.

Useful to ask at the same time: whether opening numbers and limits are available in whatever they
offer, and how far back line history goes.
